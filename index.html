Chapter 1

Lead Management

Sales ConsultantCustomer Relationship Executive

Purpose

To standardise the flow of all types of enquiries coming to the dealership from various channels.

Benefit to Dealership

  • No enquiry drop offs from OEM to call centre to dealership
  • Standardised information leading to efficient enquiry management
  • Timely appointment confirmation increases showroom visits

Benefit to Customer

  • Instant acknowledgement and personalized contact within minutes
  • A confirmed showroom visit or test drive slot
  • Customers easily transition into the physical journey

There are two types of leads received at JSW Motors dealerships:

  • Direct-to-dealer leads – digital, physical, or tele-in leads directly coming to the dealer from dealer’s website, social platforms, referrals or events.
  • OEM-to-dealer leads – JSW Motors receives leads from various physical and digital sources like:
    • JSW Motors website, CX app, automotive aggregators, walk-in, digital and tele-ins at the experience centre,
    • Paid & Organic Media & QR Code - Meta & Google Ads, Organic Search, Print Media and Events QR Code, Events and Community Registrations
    • Central Call Centre – WhatsApp chatbot, tele-in, missed call, Tele out (Sales + Service + Campaign)

Success Metrics

KPI Owner: CRE

Lead Response Time

What it measures
The average time taken to make the first contact with a customer after a lead is received
Formula
Lead Response Time=Total Time Taken for First Response / Total Leads Received

Lead Contactability (%)

What it measures
The percentage of assigned leads successfully contacted by the CRE
Formula
Lead Contactability (%)=(Leads Successfully Contacted / Total Leads Assigned)×100

Follow-up Compliance (%)

What it measures
The percentage of customer follow-ups completed as per the defined Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and scheduled timeline
Formula
Follow-up Compliance (%)=(Follow-ups Completed as per SOP/Total Follow-ups Due)×100

Leads Pending >24 / 48 / 72 Hours (%)

What it measures
The percentage of open leads that remain unattended or unresolved beyond the defined Service Level Agreement (SLA) timelines of 24, 48, or 72 hours
Formula
Leads Pending Beyond SLA (%)=(Leads Pending Beyond 24/48/72 Hours/Total Open Leads)×100
