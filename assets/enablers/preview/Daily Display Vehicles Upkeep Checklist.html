Instructions
Complete one checklist per display vehicle daily.
Recheck touch surfaces, seating and presentation after customer demonstrations; clean as required.
Do not start engines inside the showroom. Vehicle movement and powered demonstrations must follow authorised safety procedures.
Do not demonstrate a faulty feature. Restrict access to any unsafe vehicle until the issue is resolved.
Technical inspections, battery support and charging must be handled by trained, authorised personnel.
